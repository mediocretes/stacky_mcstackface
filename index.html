Pick three classes and a level, and see the best spell you can cast for each job — heal, slow, haste, nuke, damage over time, buffs — and what replaces it later. Everything is worked out from your own game files, here in your browser.

A fan-made tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by Daybreak Game Company. It ships no game data: every spell and number comes from the files you load, and they never leave this browser.
